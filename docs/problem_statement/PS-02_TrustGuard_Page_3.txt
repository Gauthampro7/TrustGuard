01
Objective
Develop a multimodal AI system capable of identifying potential synthetic media, impersonation and manipulated digital
content, and providing an explainable assessment of its authenticity and associated risk.
02
Background
Generative AI has made it easy to create convincing synthetic images, video, voice and text. This becomes a serious social
problem when manipulated content is used for:
Financial fraud
Identity impersonation
False emergency requests
Reputation attacks
Misleading public communication
Social engineering
Fabricated evidence
Coordinated misinformation
Synthetic content is no longer limited to obviously manipulated images. A scam may combine a real person's photograph, an
AI-generated voice, a fabricated document and a convincing conversation into a believable identity. The Government of
India has explicitly recognised AI-generated synthetic audio, video and text as a deepfake-related risk.
T H E Q U E S T I O N I S N O T “ I S T H I S I M A G E A I- G E N E R AT E D ? ”
“Can an AI system examine multiple pieces of evidence and determine whether the identity,
media and surrounding context are consistent and trustworthy?”

Expected Deliverables
Multimodal Input
Analyse at least two evidence types
Synthetic-Media Analysis
Identify manipulation or AI generation
Cross-Modal Verification
Compare media against identity signals
Risk Assessment
An authenticity or impersonation-risk score
Evidence-Based Explanation
Show which signals drove the verdict
Contextual Analysis
Judge context, not media in isolation
False-Positive Handling
Deal with uncertain or legitimate content
Verification Workflow
Let the user independently verify a claim
Working Demonstration
Media → analysis → risk → verification
⚠
N O S C O R E I S P R O O F
Teams must demonstrate that the system does not treat an AI-generated score as absolute proof, and provides a path for
human or independent verification. Prioritise robustness, explainability, privacy and uncertainty awareness.
E X P E C T E D O U T C O M E
A functional multimodal digital-trust prototype that helps users identify potentially manipulated media and
impersonation attempts before acting on misleading information.
