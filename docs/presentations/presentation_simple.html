01 · Why We Need This

The Everyday Threat in 2026

Scams today are no longer just bad Photoshop. They are coordinated, realistic identity attacks.

🎭 Cloned Accounts & Lookalikes

Scammers copy your profile photo, bio, and name to create lookalike handles (like adding _official or visually identical foreign letters) to scam your friends, colleagues, or followers.

🎙️ Voice & Video Impersonation

With just 10 seconds of speech from a video or podcast, AI tools can clone someone's voice and pair it with an urgent emergency request or a fake wire transfer demand.

🖼️ Stolen Content & Likeness

Creative professionals, influencers, and experts find their photos, faces, and creative work stolen, edited, and reposted across the web without credit or compensation.

💡
Grammarly & QuillBot vs. TrustGuard: Tools like Grammarly or QuillBot check if a sentence has correct commas or if an essay was written by AI. But if an attacker hacks an executive's account and writes a grammatically perfect wire-transfer scam, Grammarly gives it a 100% green checkmark! Grammarly checks grammar; TrustGuard stops identity theft, voice cloning, and fraud.

03 · Practical Feature 1

Your Personal Identity Baseline

Building a secure reference summary so the system knows what is truly 'you'.

📂 What the User Provides

  • Official Links: Your verified LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube, GitHub, or personal portfolio link.
  • Face & Photo References: A few clear profile photos to register your verified appearance.
  • Voice Sample (Optional): A quick 30-second audio clip reading a phrase to register your voice tone.
  • Writing Style Samples: A sample of past genuine posts/articles to establish your normal tone and topics.

🔒 Privacy First (Zero Data Selling)

  • No Raw Biometric Storage: Photos and audio are converted into mathematical fingerprint numbers. Raw media is never stored or used to train public AI models.
  • Encrypted & Local: Your profile data is encrypted and controlled 100% by you.
  • Instant Erasure: One click to delete your profile and wipe all baseline fingerprints anytime.
💡
Why this matters: You cannot detect an "impersonation" without knowing the authentic person. This baseline gives the system the ground truth it needs to protect you.
04 · Practical Feature 2

Fake Account & Clone Hunter

Automatically finding people pretending to be you across social platforms.

1. Multi-Tier Handle Scanner

Catches tricky impersonation variations:

  • Separator variations: Adding extra underscores like user______name vs user_name.
  • Typosquatting: Subtle character mutations (edit distance ≤ 3).
  • Lookalike letters (homoglyphs): Replacing Latin "a" with Cyrillic "а" (TR39 skeleton collision).
  • Contextual Mismatches: Unrelated handles marked neutral, avoiding false accusations.

2. Profile Picture & Bio Match

Searches for accounts that have:

  • Copied your exact profile photo or cropped versions of it.
  • Copied your bio text, company name, or title.
  • Unlinked accounts attempting to build followers using your reputation.

3. Scam Activity Alert

Flags high-risk behaviors:

  • Recently created accounts directly messaging your followers.
  • Profiles promoting crypto schemes, fake giveaways, or unauthorized paid services under your name.
📋
Actionable Output: TrustGuard gives you a direct 1-click Takedown & Report Package with pre-filled evidence links to submit to Twitter, LinkedIn, or Meta support.
05 · Practical Feature 3

Activity & Post Anomaly Detection

Detecting when an account is hacked or someone is speaking completely out-of-character.

🚨 What an Anomaly Looks Like

  • Tone & Topic Shift: A tech engineer who normally writes about Python and cloud infrastructure suddenly posts an urgent cryptocurrency investment scheme.
  • Coercion & Panic Style: Sudden heavy use of urgent, demanding words: "URGENT: Emergency wire needed! Do not call me, phone line is broken!"
  • Timing & Habit Anomaly: Unusual post bursts at 3:30 AM local time or 15 rapid automated posts within 2 minutes.
  • Life Summary Mismatch: Claims to be in London or hospital while recent verified check-ins and normal schedule confirm they are at work.

⚙️ How the System Detects It

  • Writing Habit Comparison: Checks sentence lengths, word diversity, and punctuation habits against your baseline.
  • AI-Generated Text Detection: Flags if the text reads like a generic template written by ChatGPT rather than a real human.
  • Urgency Scoring: Measures psychological pressure tactics commonly used in phishing and extortion.
🛡️
Real-World Use: Protects companies and families from the "CEO Fraud" or "Grandparent Scam" where attackers hack an account or spoof a persona to request emergency money.

Protecting creative professionals, designers, and influencers from unauthorized reuse.

🎙️ Voice Likeness Defense

For podcasters, voice artists, and speakers:

📜 Ownership Provenance

  • Proves when an image was created by a real camera vs. generated by an AI tool like Midjourney.
💼
For Professionals: Creates a digital copyright dossier that creators can use to file DMCA takedown requests or claim licensing royalties.

Real-time profile genuineness as you browse Instagram, X, or LinkedIn.

Whenever you open someone's profile page, the extension inspects public webpage elements directly:

  • Social Footprint Parsing: Reads post counts, followers, and following directly from the page layout.
  • Multi-Image AI Screening: Samples avatar and up to 3 post thumbnails, screening 2D-FFT spectra for synthetic lattice artifacts.
  • Memory Safety & Privacy: Ephemeral 64x64 downsamples strictly disposed in finally blocks; zero raw media or passwords stored.
  • SPA Route Protection: Automatically resets reference handles and observation state during route changes.

📈 Continuous Calibrated Authenticity Index (0 - 100)

Parametric model replacing crude binary flags with real profile variance:

🟢 91% Active Creator — 250 posts, 45k followers, verified links, natural image texture
🟢 82% Typical Organic User — 16 posts, 1.2k followers, balanced reciprocity, real photo
🟡 60% New / Churn Burner — 0 posts, 8 followers, follows 2.5k (ratio > 40), blank silhouette
🔴 15% Lookalike Impersonator — Cyrillic 'а', separator padding (____), or typosquat handle
⚡
Zero External API Costs: By inspecting public DOM elements on-demand, TrustGuard requires zero expensive platform APIs, operates 100% locally, and respects platform privacy boundaries.
08 · Practical Feature 6

The Multimodal Inspector & ML-1

Pretrained AI classifiers, signal-bounded uncertainty, and explainable dual ledgers.

📥 Multimodal Forensic Suite

Nine CPU Extractors & Two Pretrained AI Models:

  • RoBERTa AI-Text & Swin-v2 AI-Image Classifiers: Pinned local weights, air-gapped, zero remote network calls.
  • Audio Vocoder & Cross-Modal Sync: Detects synthetic neural vocoders and lip-sync desynchronization.
  • 2D-FFT Radial Rolloff: Catches checkerboard/lattice artifacts in synthetic generators.
  • TR39 Homoglyph Hunter: Identifies mixed-script and confusable lookalike attacks.

🔍 Signal-Bounded Epistemic Uncertainty

Key Innovation: Prevents bundle-wide dilution:

  • Per-Signal Bounding: Each signal bounded by its own usability (min(score, 1.15 - U_signal)).
  • No Dilution: A short bio or missing modality no longer outvotes a genuine forensic red flag.
  • Evaluated Uncertainty: Aggregated strictly over active extractors without penalizing abstentions.

📊 Dual Evidence Ledger: Separating Red Flags from Uncertainty

🔴 Forensic Red Flags (Definite Anomalies)
"Audio vocoder synthesis detected. Lip-sync lag > 180 ms. Handle contains Cyrillic homoglyph (U+0430)."
🔍 Uncertainty & Context (Honest Limits)
"Missing modalities marked unavailable, never zero-risk. Polished text shown as context, not false red flags."
09 · Core Hackathon Ethos

Why "No Score is Proof"

An AI score is never absolute truth. TrustGuard gives you safe ways to verify independently.

1. 5D Calibrated Trust Vector

Replaces misleading scalar ratings with five explicit dimensions:

  • Media Anomaly (synthesis)
  • Cross-Modal Discordance (lip-sync)
  • Identity Mismatch (homoglyphs)
  • Context Anomaly (urgency/style)
  • Epistemic Uncertainty (data quality)

2. Independent Verification

Instead of leaving users helpless, TrustGuard provides:

  • Ground-truth company directory lookup.
  • Steganographic canary tripwire checking.
  • Adversarial dialectic: provides explicit arguments for AND against authenticity.

3. Signed Audit Certificates

Cryptographically seals the final determination:

  • Ed25519 digital signature with embedded QR verification routing.
  • Verifiable offline or online with SHA-256 evidence hashes.
  • Downloadable as official PDF for banking, IT, or legal compliance.
🎯
Judges' Favorite: The official hackathon problem explicitly states: "Teams must demonstrate that the system does not treat an AI score as proof." TrustGuard enforces this in code, UI meters, and audit trails.
